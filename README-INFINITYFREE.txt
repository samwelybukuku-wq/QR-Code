FOUNTAIN GATE DODOMA QR INFORMATION PAGE

FILES
- index.html: the public information page to publish.
- qr-maker.html: after the page is live, enter its full public URL here to create the QR image.

PUBLISH WITH INFINITYFREE
1. Create/sign in to your InfinityFree account and add a free domain or subdomain.
2. Open the hosting account's File Manager (or connect with an FTP app).
3. Open that website's htdocs folder. Upload index.html directly into htdocs.
   The site files must be in htdocs; do not upload the ZIP as your website.
4. Open your new HTTPS website address on a phone and check the page and links.
5. Open qr-maker.html from your computer (or upload it too and visit /qr-maker.html).
   Enter the website address including https:// and the trailing slash, then create the QR.
6. Save the QR PNG, scan it with a phone, and make sure it opens your page before
   placing it on certificates. Print it with a white margin and enough size to scan.

QR destination: use the main page address, for example https://your-domain.free.nf/
Do not use a temporary preview URL. The QR image is tied to the exact URL entered.

CONTENT NOTES
The page reflects the provided PDF: school name, staff names and phone numbers,
location, office hours, website, and Instagram. The website link uses the clean
school homepage address. Please confirm staff names/numbers and the location pin
before printing a large batch of certificates.

The QR maker uses an online QR image service when you click Create. It sends the
URL you enter to that service to generate the QR PNG. Do not enter private data.
